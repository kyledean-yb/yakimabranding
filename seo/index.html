What We Do

What Is Search Engine Optimization?

Most people who search online don't wade through pages of results — they click the top few. SEO is the science of getting your business there. We handle the ever-changing algorithm so you don't have to.

A Personalized Plan for Every Client
No two businesses get the same strategy. We start with your services, your service area and your competitors, then pick the keywords and pages that will bring in the customers you actually want.
More Visibility in Google Search
We work to get you onto page one for the searches that matter most, so people see your business before they see a competitor's. Most clicks go to the top few results, and that's where we aim.
Show Up Everywhere People Look
Google, the map pack, Apple Maps, Yelp, local directories and AI tools. We keep your name, address and details consistent everywhere, so customers and search engines can trust what they find.
A Website Google Can Read Easily
Fast pages, clean structure, schema markup, and fixes for anything that keeps Google from crawling or indexing your site. It's behind the scenes work that helps everything else rank.
Rank for More of the Right Searches
We find the exact phrases your customers type, including the specific ones that are easier to win, and build pages around them so you show up for more of them month after month.
Traffic That Turns Into Calls
More rankings bring more visitors, but what matters is what they do next. We track calls and form fills so you can see which traffic is actually bringing in business.

How It Works

What Happens After You Sign

SEO has a reputation for being a black box. Here's exactly what we do, in order, and what you'll see along the way.

  1. 1
    Month 1

    Audit, Tracking and Fixes

    Audit. We look at your rankings, your website's technical health, your Google Business Profile and your top competitors. You get our findings in plain English.

    Tracking. Before we change anything, we make sure conversions and rankings are being measured. Otherwise there's no way to prove what worked.

    Fixes. The technical problems, profile cleanup and directory listings come first. They're the quickest wins and everything else depends on them.

  2. 2
    Month 2 onward

    Content

    We build or rewrite the pages that match what your customers search for: service pages, location pages, and clear answers to the questions they ask before they call.

  3. 3
    Every month

    Reporting

    A report showing rankings, traffic, and conversions with recommendations and observations each month.

SEO vs. PPC

Which Is Right for Your Business?

Pay-per-click (PPC) advertising offers immediate visibility and control, while SEO builds organic, sustainable traffic over time. Many businesses benefit from both. Get in touch with YB to find the right mix for your goals.

SEO
  • ✓Long-term, sustainable organic traffic
  • ✓Builds authority and trust with Google
  • ✓Compounds in value — results grow over time
  • ✓Ranks in both traditional and AI search results
PPC / Google Ads
  • ✓Immediate visibility at the top of Google
  • ✓Full control over budget and targeting
  • ✓Great for promotions, launches, and events
  • ✓Measurable ROI on every dollar spent
